THE DIVERSITY OF ANAPHYLAXIS

The anaphylaxis may manifest as local reaction of skin or mucosa. Depending on location, this may look like urticaria (hives), vasomotor rhinitis (hay fever), atopic sinusitis, atopic bronchial asthma, allergic diarrhea, and angioedema, atopic dermatitis etc.

The most dangerous acute manifestation of atopic reactivity is systemic anaphylaxis, resulted in a kind of shock. We shall discuss local forms first, and systemic one – later.
