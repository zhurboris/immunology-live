THE ETIOLOGY OF ALLERGIC REACTIONS

The cause of allergy is abnormal interaction of immune system and allergens. The dose and kind of allergen, terms of its persistence within APC, route of allergen penetration, general status of immune system – all are of some significance for determination of response scale and character. But, the most powerful are individual genetic peculiarities of a concrete organism. The allergy as a medical phenomenon, in majority of cases is composed of excessive and poorly regulated immune response, stimulating too intensive and inadequately arranged inflammation. The offensive allergen is just a trigger, pushing the initial stage of the process, which could not occur without genetically determined individual abnormalities of the body reaction. This individual genetics makes a reaction towards concrete antigen redundant and its regulation deranged. Millions of people contact with penicillin, but only few suffer from penicillin-derived anaphylaxis.
