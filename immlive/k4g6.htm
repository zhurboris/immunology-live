AUTOIMMUNE DISORDERS DUE TO ABNORMAL EXPRESSION OF MHC II GLYCOPROTEINS AND COSTIMULATORY MOLECULES

T-helper is able to recognize a sequential epitope only if it is presented in complex with MHC class 2 glycoproteins. Moreover, the presence of costimulatory molecules is compulsory. Without them exposure of T-cells to autoantigens make them anergic. It means that to a certain extent the silence of autoreactive T clones depends on normal absence of MHC class 2 molecules and costimulatory molecules on the surface of self somatic cells, excerpt some immunocompetent APCs directly involved in immune cooperation. But, principally all cells of the body possess with the same set of genes, so all of them theoretically can express Class 2 molecules and costimulatory triggers under certain special conditions. G. -F. Bottazzo et al. (1983) have demonstrated that abnormal “ectopic” expression of these molecules may cause autoallergy. In the situation of aberrant MHC class 2 expression target cells present their own antigens to autoreactive T-helpers. This mechanism is proven to be involved in autoimmune thyreoiditis, Graves’ disease, primary insulin dependent diabetes mellitus, demyelinizing CNS disorders and in experimental allergic encephalomyelitis of rats. Abnormal expression can be triggered by IFNa and IFNg , as well as by TNF. The combined action of these cytokines is especially provocative. Infection and resulting inflammation can activate cells to express MHC class 2 and costimulatory molecules.

Most frequently, it is thought to be caused by viral infections, predisposing to autoallergy. Unfortunately, jatrogenic induction of this type of autoallergy is also possible: autoimmune thyroiditis and other autoallergic disorders are registered among the patients treated by interferons due to chronic viral hepatitis C. Some functional defects of target cells may, probably, facilitate the MHC aberrant expression: in strain of obese chickens, spontaneously developing autoimmune thyroiditis and in some patients with Hashimoto’s thyroiditis the uptake of iodine by thyroid gland in latent stage of disease is abnormally high and this phenomenon antecedes the aberrant expression of MHC class 2 glycoproteins.
